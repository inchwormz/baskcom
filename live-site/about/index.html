Bask curates furniture and home objects for New Zealand living. Simple forms, warm finishes and useful pieces that fit around your day.

Our story

A home comes together one piece at a time. Bask is built around choosing furniture that earns its place: useful storage, comfortable rooms and a table to gather around.

We’re curators. We bring together a selection across bedroom, living, dining and home office, with a focus on practical details and calm interiors.

If you need help choosing a size, checking availability or arranging delivery, contact Bask. We’ll help you work through the details before you order.
